Check before you share

  • Search for the original source. Real events are usually reported by more than one account.
  • Watch the edges of the face, the teeth and the hairline for flicker or blur.
  • Check whether lip movement matches the sound, and whether blinking looks natural.
  • Be most careful with videos that make you angry or afraid. They spread fastest.
  • If you are unsure, do not share it.
